# Visual system

The supplied Alpaca screenshot is the reference, excluding its browser chrome
and product identity.

## Type and color

The system font stack starts with `-apple-system` and `BlinkMacSystemFont`.
No proprietary font files are bundled. Regular 400 and medium 500 carry 12 px
metadata, 13 px secondary copy, 14 px body/navigation and 24 px screen titles.
Tracking is -0.15 px.

Colors are white, #292929 primary, #5D5D5D secondary and #9E9E9E tertiary.
Shell dividers are #F0F0F0. Cyan marks a recent local entry only.

## Geometry

Desktop uses a 64 px top bar and 240 px sidebar. Content is left-aligned inside
a 640 px maximum-width region. The three Mobile features share a single card:
16 px corners, 24 px padding and 20 px icons. Navigation has 14 px icons and
8 px corners. Primary actions are pill-shaped. A barely visible corner dot field
leaves the main area calm.

Below 760 px the sidebar is toggled. Text and dialogs remain within the viewport.
Controls either perform a real local action or explain an unavailable connection.
